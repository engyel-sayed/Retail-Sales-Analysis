# 📊 Retail Sales & Catalog Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
An interactive Business Intelligence (BI) dashboard built to analyze retail product catalogs, stock availability, and sales performance. The data was extracted directly from a **SQL Database** to model metrics and deliver actionable insights for inventory control and product performance.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics & Visuals
- **Catalog Overview:** Total Catalogs, Total Products, and Average Products per Catalog.
- **Category Analytics:** Breakdown of product distribution across catalog categories.
- **Stock Tracking:** Real-time visibility into product availability and stock status.
- **Product Performance:** Identification of top-performing products and sales trends.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Source:** SQL Database (Data Extraction & Queries)
- **BI & Visualization:** Tableau
